Cable Connection

Connect each colored endpoint to its matching endpoint. Plan your route carefully: cables cannot overlap, and you can only move horizontally or vertically.

How to play

  1. Select either endpoint of a colored cable.
  2. Click an adjacent cell to extend the cable.
  3. Continue until you reach the matching endpoint.
  4. Connect all three colors to complete the level.

Tip: If you make a mistake, click an earlier cell in your active path to undo the cells after it. Use Reset level to start over.
